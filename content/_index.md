Wild Fenn are an Indie/Folk band based in Devon, UK. Songwriters Luke Engel and Jess Illingworth love writing songs with beautiful harmonies. Their lyrics often take inspiration from nature and lead the listener to wild places. Wild Fenn are influenced by the likes of Gregory Alan Isokov, Roo Panes, Frank Turner and The Lone Bellow and create music that is both upbeat and grounding.

## EP Launch - A Place To Call Home - Cafe Momus, Plymouth 25/11/26 @ 7pm

![Hourglass](place-to-call-home.png)

**Wild Fenn EP Launch Gig @ Cafe Momus, Plymouth**
*Wednesday 25 November 2026, 7pm - 10pm*

A full band show to celebrate the release of our second EP, ‘A Place To Call Home’.

Get ready for an evening of indie-folk music as we celebrate the launch of our second EP!

Our second EP 'A Place To Call Home' will be coming out on 25th November. They're a collection of songs themed around finding home, whether that's in a place, or with people. We've collaborated with our friend, and incredibly talented producer, Jack Cookson on these songs. He has worked his magic on them and we are delighted with how they turned out. 

We are playing a full band gig on the 25th November at Cafe Momus in Plymouth to celebrate.


Kocapoli will be supporting us, and we might bring up some guests to the stage, so it's looking to be a fun evening!

Twin sisters Alice & Kit and brother Billy Twamley have been playing together since they were little. Kocapoli blend Folk, Soul, and Alternative across and within their songs, weaving irresistible bloodline harmonies with funky and positive energy.

Getting to/from the venue:

Car: There is some on street parking nearby and a car park within walking distance.

Bus: The venue is close to the city centre so is accessible via public transport.

Train: The Plymouth Railway station is about a 15 minute walk from the venue.

## Latest Single Release - Hestia

{{< figure src="hestia.png" alt="Hestia sitting by a fire watching people move" class="mx-auto" >}}

Hestia was inspired by the character from the Percy Jackson books, and is written as an anthem for quieter voices. It's not always easy to be a quiet presence in a room full of big personalities. Hestia is a reflection on knowing your worth, and recognising that there's strength in being calm and steady. 

We recorded the base track for this song at the end of a day in the studio. We had just enough time to record one take, so the main vocal that you hear is the first and only take we recorded!
