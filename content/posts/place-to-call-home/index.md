## EP Launch - A Place To Call Home

{{< figure src="place-to-call-home.png" alt="An hourglass with a grey city scape above draining into a garden oasis below" class="mx-auto" >}}

Wild Fenn's second EP 'A Place To Call Home' will be coming out on 25th November. They're a collection of songs themed around finding home, whether that's in a place, or with people. We've collaborated with our friend, and incredibly talented producer, Jack Cookson on these songs. He has worked his magic on them and we are delighted with how they turned out. 

We are playing a full band gig on the 25th November at Cafe Momus in Plymouth to celebrate. Kocapoli will be supporting us, and we might bring up some guests to the stage, so it's looking to be a fun evening!
